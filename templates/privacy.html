Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash) provided during registration.
  • Expense entries — all financial transactions you manually log, including amounts, categories, dates, and any notes you attach.
  • Receipt uploads — any images or files you optionally attach to an expense entry.
  • Usage data — basic interaction data such as pages visited, used solely to maintain and improve app functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically, your data is used to:

  • Display your logged expenses and transaction history within the app.
  • Calculate spending analytics, category breakdowns, and budget summaries.
  • Render charts and visualisations to help you understand your financial patterns.
  • Authenticate your account and keep your session secure.
  • Respond to support requests or account-related queries.

We do not use your financial data for any purpose beyond providing the core features of the application.

3. Data Storage and Security

All your financial logs, expense entries, and account credentials are stored securely. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We apply standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we are committed to maintaining appropriate safeguards for the sensitivity of personal financial information.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial tracking data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties for commercial purposes. Your expense history and account information are never used to serve targeted advertising. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • When required to comply with a legal obligation or lawful government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of Spendly or its users.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data. At any time, you may:

  • Access your data — view all expense entries and account information from within the app.
  • Delete individual entries — remove any specific expense record from your history.
  • Delete your account — permanently remove your account and all associated data through your account settings. Deletion is irreversible.

If you require assistance exercising any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 7.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application or applicable law. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of Spendly after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
